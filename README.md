# Yiğit Denktaş

**Architect by training. Computational designer and software developer by practice.**  
Cologne, Germany · [yigitdenktas.com ↗](https://www.yigitdenktas.com/)

---

I work between architecture and software: the physical systems we design, the digital tools we use to design them, and the people on either side of the interface.

At work, I develop **BIM and geospatial software** for the built environment. In my independent practice, I explore **spatial computing, human–AI interaction, and design through making**.

### What I'm interested in

**01 / Space as a system**  
Geometry, topology, parametric design and the constraints that turn a model into something buildable.

**02 / Software for the physical world**  
Spatial data, design tools and interfaces that make complex information useful to people doing real work.

**03 / Human–machine collaboration**  
AI as an instrument for exploration and critique, with authorship and judgment remaining human.

**04 / Experiments beyond the screen**  
Digital fabrication, material studies, interactive publishing and whatever else gives an idea a useful physical form.
